DS-10 Sample Injector

Turns your Korg DS-10's four drums into sample players: up to 128 of your own sounds, picked per drum with five knobs in the drum editor, with looping and an attack / hold / fade envelope. Everything happens in this browser; nothing is uploaded.

1 Your DS-10 ROM

Drop your .nds here, or click to choose it. Supported: DS-10 Europe, USA and Japan.

2 Samples

Drop WAV files here (or click). A name starting with a number, like 001 kick.wav, goes to that place; others fill the next free place.

Each sound is converted to mono 32,768 Hz and cut to 1 second. Loop points in a WAV's smpl chunk make it loop; a loop that doesn't fit in a second is shortened with a crossfade. 8-bit halves the size.

Library 0 of 128 places
On the DS: VCO1 wave picks the group, VCO2 wave the row; balance, VCO2 pitch and sync the column.
